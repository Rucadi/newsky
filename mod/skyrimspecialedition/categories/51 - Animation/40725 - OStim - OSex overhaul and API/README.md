OSA is an amazing mod, but let's face it: it has some shortcomings from the gameplay side of things.
  
 
  
OStim does many things, the 4 main being the following. Firstly it hooks into OSA and patches in the bread and butter of this mod: the Stimulation System. Secondly it brings about numerous quality of life fixes such as automatic undressing, automatic light control, and more. Third, it lets you set scenes to be automatically controlled hands-free like other frameworks. Lastly it simplifies OSA's rather obscure API so that modders can control OSA easily through OStim.
  
  
What began as a project to breathe new life into OSA has turned into something that aims to be the best sex mod in Skyrim, and to offer a more modern, refined, and advanced sex experience over Sexlab.
  
 
  
The Stimulation System
  
 
  
Have you used OSA and felt like it was meant to be more of a screenshot tool rather than something integrated into gameplay?
  
 
  
The Stimulation System is a new take on sex scenes in Skyrim built right on top of OSA. Characters in a OSA scene now have a new stat: "Excitement". This is essentially a measure of how close a character is to orgasm. This stat rises and falls over the course of a scene depending on what you are doing in it, in a natural feeling way. For example, blowjobs will generally increase male excitement while females giving the blowjob will get less of an excitement boost, intercourse will generally increase the excitement of both characters involved, and so on. Once a character's excitement reaches max, they will orgasm. Once the lead character in an animation orgasms (usually the male), the animation will end and return to gameplay on it's own, similar to the other sex animation framework.
  
 
  
The actual formula that controls how much Excitement a character gets is fairly complex and simulates a number of body parts and systems. Each "sex act" has it's own base Excitement values, so some acts bring certain characters more pleasure than others. Systems such as natural vaginal lubrication which increases as arousal rises, are also simulated. This means that females may enjoy intercourse more after they've been *loosened up* with female-centric sex acts. With all of these systems interacting, you'll sometimes find that foreplay is necessary if you want your partner to orgasm before you do. Making an NPC orgasm during sex will increase their relationship rank with you, which has a several effects in the base game, and more with other mods(this feature can be adjusted in the MCM). Of course the speed of the animation in OSA also plays a large role, and now actors will automatically speed up the animation on their own once their excitement passes a certain threshold (this feature can also be adjusted from the MCM). You can track characters' excitement values with the onscreen bars.
  
 
  
This system makes OSA feel like an actual sexual interaction between you and an NPC, instead of like playing with a pair of dolls.
  
  
Full-auto Mode
  
Now in OStim 2, you can optionally enable a full auto mode and let your sex scenes play out in front of you, hands free! To make things even better, animations in these full auto scenes aren't just randomly selected, OStim gathers data about your surroundings and your partner to generate unique scenes that feel natural.
  
  
QOL Changes
  
  
Clunkiness be gone!
  
Within the OStim MCM you can now choose to force all actors to undress when an animation starts, choosing between having the clothes simply unequipped, or to have OSA play it's special undressing animations at the beginning automatically. Optionally instead you can also set OStim to simply unequip clothes when needed, leaving them on until something like intercourse starts.  OStim also bring automatic redressing, optionally fully animating the redressing. Additionally you can set up OSA's face and ass lights to turn themselves on at the start of every scene, or if you want, only in dark areas. And of course as detailed above, characters can automatically control a scene's speed themselves and end the scene at an appropriate time on their own, meaning you can spend less time micromanaging scenes. On top of that, beds are fully supported, and you can set OStim to enter freecam mode automatically with customizable FOV and camera speed.

OStim API
  
  
OSA's API isn't often used, because it's not well documented, fairly limited, and several features of it are broken. The OStim api sits on top of OSA's, and expands it significantly. Additionally I've replaced OSA's starter script with my own, so that mods that already exist that start OSA scenes will be rerouted through OStim instead!
  
Learn how to use the API in the articles tab of this mod page

Compatibility and addons
  
Mods that are compatible with OSA already are compatible and start OStim instead of normal OSA. A small amount of these mods may have a few specific features missing. If you can confirm a mod is working with OStim, let me know about and I'll add it down below
  
  
Mods that use events from other sex mods like sexlab will not receive events from this mod. For example, a pregnancy mod that has a chance to cause pregnancy during a sexlab animation. Due to the complex nature of sexlab, it is very difficult for me to develop a one size fits all solution to force this mod to be compatible as a sexlab replacement. However, on a case-by-case basis, building a patch to translate OStim events into sexlab ones is possible and fairly easy. I will leave it up to the community to build patches for individual mods. If you build a patch and want it hosted here, I can do so. Just send it to me
  
However, on this mod's LL forum thread, another user has created an experimental SLAL events bridge.
  
  
Compatible with controllers, be sure to map your keys in OSA
  
  
                                                                                      
  
 
  
Installing
  
Install all requirements listed on the download page. If using a mod manager, make sure this mod's files overwrite OSA's, since it has a custom starter script
  
Make sure to use the specific versions of OSA and OSex listed at the top of this page, the other versions will not work.
  
AE users, OStim was made on SE so you will have to find AE versions of the requirements. Also install the AE patch next to the OSA and OSex links. 
  
  
Afterwards, you can map the sex starter key in the MCM. Note that if you instead start scenes through OSA's menu, OStim will not load on top of it.
  
  
Note: installing OStim into a save where OSA is already installed does not work, make sure that OSA and OStim start up at the same time. Uninstall and save clean OSA if you have it installed without OStim.

Controls
  
If you never used OSA before, you'll probably be unfamiliar with how to actually navigate the navtab during sex. Here are the controls. Remember that if the controls are too daunting you can enable full auto-mode in the MCM at any time for a sexlab-like hands free experience
  
  
Default controls (these are remappable):
  
Numpad 4, 5, 6, 8 - left/down/right/up, similar to a WASD setup
  
Numpad 7 - Select
  
Numpad 9 - Change tab (when applicable)
  
Numpad Enter - open OSA's main menu at any time
  
  
No numpad on your PC? Hold F8 for up to 10 seconds to activate emergency remapping
  
  
  
Frequent Issues/questions
  
>Characters keep redressing themselves mid scene for no reason
  
Most likely you've installed 3BA or BHUNP body. By default these mods bind the same keys as OSA. The solution is to rebind the hotkeys in the 3BA/BHUNP MCM.
  
  
>What is the correct load order
  
1. OSA;
  
2. OSA dependencies;
  
3. OStim;
  
  
If you use multiple animation packs, the load order for them does not matter if you use a compatibility patch and load it after the animation packs.
  
  
>How do I get rid of the spanking dialogues
  
OStim does not add any dialogue options, those options are from BBLS. That version is also very old you should remove it and use that author's new Naughty Voices and OPrivacy 
  
  
>The excitement bars lock up for me or go extremely slow. I've already done a save clean
  
Your papyrus script engine is overloaded - the way to fix this actually not to remove script heavy mods. Instead, remove graphics-heavy mods to raise your framerate. Papyrus Engine performance is heavily dependent on framerate, and based on my tests I cannot recommend you play scenes with an average framerate lower than 45. This extends beyond OStim and into modded skyrim in general.
  
  
>Doesn't work, plus the MCM is empty
  
Exit without saving, and move papyrusutils under OSA
  
  
>OSA/OSex don't seem to start at all. Also, when I hit enter on the numpad, OSA's main menu doesn't appear.
  
OSA is a skyui widget. Some alternate start mods and possibly other mods that tweak skyui widgets can stop OSA from being loaded up. Try completing your alternate start and/or removing mods that tweak skyui widgets like iHud
  
Every single time you load a save, a small spinning golden circle should appear at the top of the hud just to the right of where the vanilla compass is for a brief moment. This means OSA initialized correctly. If you don't see this circle, you have an OSA related issue

>Does this work in VR?
  
No it doesn't. OSA was never made with VR in mind so it needs to be tweaked and I don't have a VR headset. I can't do VR specific stuff unless I have a dev partner with a VR headset or someone sends me some kind of VR equipment. I would like for users to be able to move their hands and bodies to control, or "act" in the scene. OSex animations contain position data that could make a fully physically interactive setup possible, and body part "stats" are already defined and used in OStim, so only hitboxes would have to be made.
  
If you have VR stuff you are free to make pull requests on github.
  
  
>The characters are misalligned!
  
Misalignment is fairly common with sex frameworks. 
  
There are three types of misallignment:
  
  
Huge (more than a meter) misallignment
  
This is usually caused by HDT-SMP when used with 3bbb. See the bugs section below for more information
  
  
Cock misalignment
  
Cocks come in a ton of sizes. OAlign can help fix this.
  
  
Small misalignment
  
Sometimes characters are just an inch or two off. This is because the animations were made for a different pair of bodies than what you're using. Additionally, each animation pack was likely made for a different set as well. OAlign can fix this
